{% if project.title == "Campus Skillswap" %}

Business Problem

College students often have skills in their discipline and needs outside the scope of their knowledge. The goal of Campus Skillswap is to allow students to bless their community through their skills.

Key Features

Personal and public dashboards, with availability, cost, services, and contact information included in each post.

My Role

I worked alongside Codex within Visual Studio Code to develop the web app. I prompted Codex to develop the structure, spent time changing the aesthetics, and provided more detailed prompts for AI to create an artificial database of pseudo-students using the dashboard.

Biggest Challenge

Prompting the AI to create an aesthetically pleasing dashboard. The style Codex automatically opts for is very outdated.

What I Learned

Specificity in prompting is crucial, but even a specific prompt may not be interpreted as desired. Adjusting prompts based on what AI produced is unavoidable.
